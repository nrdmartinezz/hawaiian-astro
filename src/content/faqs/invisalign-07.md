---
question: "How should I clean Invisalign aligners without damaging them?"
topic: "Invisalign"
pages:
  - "/treatments/invisalign/"
  - "/treatments/invisalign/adult/"
  - "/treatments/invisalign/teen/"
order: 7
---

Use a soft-bristled toothbrush, liquid soap, and lukewarm or cool water. Skip hot water, which can warp the plastic. Follow the instructions for your appliance.
