question: 'How does the orthodontist decide whether braces or clear aligners are a better fit?'
topic: 'Braces'
pages:
  - '/treatments/braces/'
order: 14
---

We consider the bite, the movements needed, oral health, lifestyle, goals, and whether you can follow instructions for removable aligners. The first visit includes an exam, X-rays, and that discussion.
