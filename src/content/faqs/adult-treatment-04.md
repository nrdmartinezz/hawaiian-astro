---
question: 'How can adults compare braces, self-ligating brackets, and Invisalign?'
topic: 'Adult treatment'
pages:
  - '/treatments/adult-treatment/'
order: 4
---

Braces stay on the teeth. Self-ligating brackets use built-in doors instead of elastic ties. Invisalign uses removable clear trays. The better fit depends on the movements required, how discreet you want to be, oral health, lifestyle, and whether you can follow instructions.
