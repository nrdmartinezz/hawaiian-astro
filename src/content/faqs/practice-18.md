question: 'How does Hawaiian Smiles help patients handle an orthodontic emergency?'
topic: 'Practice'
pages:
  - '/treatments/emergency/'
order: 18
---

Call us for a loose bracket, a broken or poking wire, or general tooth pain. For trauma, infection or swelling, severe pain, uncontrolled bleeding, or anything that could be serious, go to emergency care first, then contact us.
