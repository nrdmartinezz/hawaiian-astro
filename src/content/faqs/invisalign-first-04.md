---
question: 'How does Invisalign First compare with traditional braces for a young child?'
topic: 'Invisalign First'
pages:
  - '/treatments/invisalign/children/'
order: 4
---

Invisalign First is removable and has no brackets or wires, which can make brushing and flossing easier. It still needs consistent wear, and it is not right for every child.
