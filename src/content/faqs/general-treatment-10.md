---
question: 'What should I do if an orthodontic appliance breaks or begins to bother my mouth?'
topic: 'General treatment'
pages:
  - '/treatments/'
order: 10
---

Call the office. Loose or broken brackets, bands, and wires, and wires that poke, are common problems we can help with. Orthodontic wax can cover an irritated spot until you are seen.
