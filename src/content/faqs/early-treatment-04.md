---
question: 'What dental or jaw concerns may lead Hawaiian Smiles to recommend Phase 1 treatment?'
topic: 'Early treatment'
pages:
  - '/treatments/early-treatment/'
order: 4
---

We look at severe crossbite, crowding, protruding teeth, a severe underbite, narrow arches, and the effects of thumb sucking, tongue thrust, or mouth breathing. We decide whether treatment fits that child.
