question: 'Can I remove Invisalign aligners to eat, drink, brush, and floss?'
topic: 'Invisalign'

Yes. Take them out to eat, drink, brush, and floss, then put them back in. Keep them in except for meals, drinks other than plain water, and cleaning your teeth.
