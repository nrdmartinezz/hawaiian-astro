question: 'What is Invisalign First, and which children may be evaluated for it?'
topic: 'Invisalign First'

  - '/treatments/invisalign/children/'

---

Invisalign First is clear-aligner treatment for developing smiles, used as a Phase 1 option. We may consider it for younger children, often around ages six to ten. Candidacy depends on the exam.
