---
question: "How is Invisalign First used as a Phase 1 treatment for children around ages six to ten?"
topic: "Invisalign First"
pages:
  - "/treatments/invisalign/children/"
order: 2
---

It can guide developing teeth, address some bite problems, make room for permanent teeth, and make a later phase more efficient. Ages six to ten is a general range, not a guarantee that a child qualifies.
