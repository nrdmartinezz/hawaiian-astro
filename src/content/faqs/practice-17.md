We are open Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Existing patients should give 48 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule. A $55 fee applies without that notice. After two missed or rescheduled new-patient consultations, a $150 deposit may be required to reserve the next visit.
