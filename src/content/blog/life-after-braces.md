Teeth remember where they used to sit. After braces or Invisalign, a retainer is what keeps the new positions while bone and gums settle. Skip it, and crowding and rotation start to creep back — sometimes within weeks.

## What we fit

Most patients leave with a removable clear retainer for nights, and some also get a thin wire bonded behind the front teeth. We will tell you which mix matches your bite. Wear is full-time at first, then nights — often for years, and for many people, indefinitely a few nights a week.

## Care that actually lasts

- Rinse retainers after you take them out. Brush them with a soft brush and clear soap, not hot water or toothpaste.
- Keep the case in your bag. A napkin on a cafeteria tray is how retainers disappear.
- Pets chew them. Nightstands are not a safe parking spot.
- Call us if a retainer cracks, warps, or feels tight in a new way. A replacement is cheaper than starting treatment over.

## The first weeks without braces

Expect a day or two of extra saliva and a lisp while your tongue learns the retainer. Sensitivity fades. If a spot rubs, we can adjust it — do not trim the plastic yourself.
