### Why Retention Is Essential After Braces

Once your braces are removed, your teeth are not instantly set in stone. The bone and tissues around your teeth need time to stabilize. Without proper retention, teeth naturally tend to shift back toward their original positions — a process called relapse. Retainers help to hold your teeth in their new, corrected alignment while your smile settles into its healthy new form. At Hawaiian Smiles Orthodontics, we emphasize the importance of this phase as much as your initial treatment.

Retention isn’t just about wearing a retainer — it’s about committing to protecting your investment in your smile. Whether you had traditional braces, Invisalign, or self-ligating brackets, the retention phase is critical.

### How to Care for Your Retainer

Proper care for your retainer prolongs its life and keeps your smile fresh. Clean your removable retainers daily using a gentle toothbrush and mild soap or a retainer cleaner recommended by our team. Avoid hot water as it can warp plastic retainers. For fixed retainers, maintaining excellent oral hygiene with floss threaders and regular dental cleanings is essential to prevent plaque buildup.

If your retainer is lost or damaged, contact us immediately. Wearing your retainer consistently is crucial to preventing relapse, and we can help you get a replacement quickly.

### Preventing Relapse: Tips for Long-Term Success

Relapse can be frustrating, but it’s almost always preventable with the right care. In addition to wearing your retainer as prescribed, regular follow-up visits at Hawaiian Smiles Orthodontics allow us to monitor your teeth and make adjustments if necessary. Be mindful of habits such as nail-biting, chewing on hard objects, or teeth grinding, as these can affect tooth alignment.

Remember, retention is a lifelong commitment. Some patients wear retainers nightly for years or even indefinitely to preserve their smile.
