## Why Adult Orthodontics Matters More Than Ever

Orthodontic treatment isn’t just about aesthetics; it’s an important part of improving your overall oral health. Misaligned teeth can contribute to issues such as gum disease, tooth decay, uneven wear, and even jaw discomfort. Although adult bone structures are fully developed, modern orthodontics offers highly effective treatment options tailored to adult lifestyles and unique dental needs.

At Hawaiian Smiles Orthodontics, we specialize in adult orthodontic care that prioritizes comfort, safety, and lasting results. It’s never too late to invest in your smile and your overall well-being.

## Modern Treatment Options Designed for Adult Lifestyles

Today’s orthodontic options are more discreet and comfortable than ever. Whether you prefer removable aligners or fixed appliances, our team will customize a treatment plan that fits your goals and daily routine.

| Treatment Type         | Visibility                   | Comfort Level | Treatment Duration | Maintenance                              |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------- | ------------------ | ---------------------------------------- |
| Invisalign             | Nearly Invisible             | High          | 12–18 Months       | Removable; requires consistent wear      |
| Self-Ligating Brackets | Low-Profile Metal or Ceramic | Moderate      | 12–24 Months       | Fixed appliance with fewer adjustments   |
| Traditional Braces     | More Visible                 | Moderate      | 18–30 Months       | Fixed appliance requiring routine visits |

Invisalign clear aligners remain one of our most popular choices for adults because they provide a virtually invisible way to straighten teeth without disrupting your lifestyle. For more complex corrections, self-ligating brackets and traditional braces offer highly effective solutions with greater control over tooth movement.

## Health Benefits Beyond Straight Teeth

A beautiful smile is just one benefit of orthodontic treatment. Proper alignment improves oral health by making brushing and flossing easier, reducing the risk of cavities and gum disease. Correcting bite issues can also minimize excessive wear on teeth and relieve strain on the jaw joints.

At Hawaiian Smiles Orthodontics, our approach is focused on comprehensive health. By improving your bite and alignment, we help create a foundation for healthier teeth and gums for years to come.
