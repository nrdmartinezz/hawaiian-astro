Summer in Hawaiʻi means beach weeks, neighbor-island trips, camps, and flights to the mainland. If you have finished treatment at Hawaiian Smiles Orthodontics, the [retainer](/treatments/retainers/) still comes with you. Teeth move when nothing is holding them, and a week of skipped nights is enough for a retainer to feel tight when you put it back in.

## Wear it every night

Most patients move from full-time wear to nights. “Nights” means every night, including the ones in a hotel or a cabin. If the retainer is only in your mouth at bedtime, the morning routine is what keeps it from getting left on Oʻahu while you are in Kona.

After you take it out and brush it with cool water and a soft brush, put it straight into the case. Then put the case with your pajamas or on the pillow you will use that night. The case is the reminder. A napkin on a breakfast tray is how retainers get thrown away.

A few other things that matter more when you are away from home:

- Heat warps plastic. Do not leave the case on a dashboard, a sunny lanai, or next to a hot drink.
- Pets chew retainers. A nightstand in someone else’s house is not a safe spot.
- If it cracks, warps, or suddenly feels tight in a new way, call us. A replacement is cheaper than starting treatment over.

Have a good summer. Bring the case.
