Adults are a regular part of the schedule at Hawaiian Smiles Orthodontics, and many of them are in [Invisalign](/treatments/invisalign/adult/). You often cannot tell. The trays are clear, and they come out for meals, photos, and a presentation.

Some adults want to skip the idea that braces are only for kids. Others want to avoid brackets at work. The American Association of Orthodontists reported that from 1994 to 2010 the number of adults 18 and older in treatment rose 58 percent, from about 680,000 to 1.1 million a year. A large share of those patients chose clear aligners because they are discreet and because eating does not come with a list of foods to avoid.

## What adults notice compared with braces

Invisalign is not automatically the shorter or simpler option. [Dr. Satya Nayak, Dr. Alyssa Shaikh, and Dr. Nicole Fernandez](/about/doctors/) pick it when the bite and your routine both fit. When it does, these are the differences patients care about:

- The plan is mapped in 3D before the first tray, so the sequence of movements is specific instead of a rough estimate adjusted at each visit.
- You change trays at home on the schedule we give you, so you spend less time in the chair than you would with braces adjustments.
- There are no brackets bonded to the enamel.
- The trays are clear. Most people you see during the day will not know you are in treatment.

You still wear them 20 to 22 hours a day. Taking them out for every coffee and putting them back in is the part that makes or breaks the timeline. We are an Invisalign Diamond Plus provider, which means a high volume of adult cases and access to the current aligner features, including attachments and elastics when a bite needs them.
