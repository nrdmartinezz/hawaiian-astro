## What Determines the Cost of Braces and Orthodontic Treatments?

Regarding your specialized orthodontic care, there is no definite answer about prices. This is why it's always necessary to attend a consultation with an experienced orthodontics specialist that will guide you to understand how the many factors affect the overall cost of treatment. Here we include some examples of those factors.

### Treatment Duration

This factor is associated with the severity of your orthodontic bite disorder. The duration of treatment ranges between one to three years. The greater the complexity and length of the treatment, the higher its final cost. Please keep in mind that everyone's conditions will vary, and some appliances may work faster than others.

### Geographical Location

This factor depends upon the Orthodontics Specialist’s discretion in a significant way. However, you can expect to pay higher if the [Orthodontics Office](/contact/) is located in a major city, whereas you might pay less at Orthodontic Offices located in rural areas.

### The Complexity of the Procedure

As previously mentioned, this factor is highly associated with treatment duration. In some cases, braces have to correct misaligned teeth by closing extensive gaps. Also, your jaws might be misplaced and require a more complex type of treatment. These cases have an impact on the cost you might expect to pay for orthodontic treatment. Some treatment plans rely on various stages, while others might call for cooperation with other specialists, such as periodontists and oral surgeons. Please remember that customized treatment plans will be more expensive as they work on your expectations and needs, not on general averages.

## Cost of Braces per Type

### Traditional Metal Braces

After an evaluation, your orthodontics specialist will inform you about the need to use an orthodontic appliance to correct a bite disorder. Also, depending on your specific treatment needs and budget, you might get traditional braces for [kids](/treatments/early-treatment/) or [adults](/treatments/adult-treatment/). This type of Orthodontic treatment is less expensive than other new types of braces.

We use stainless steel to manufacture highly-resistant brackets and archwires. The design helps perform simultaneous movements, but they're much more noticeable in your mouth when installed. This option is always the most affordable but not the most esthetically appealing.

### [Self-Ligating Braces](/treatments/braces/)

Self-ligating braces are also a brackets-and-wires type of orthodontic system. However, they do have several advantages over traditional braces; these are:

- Shorter orthodontic visits;
- Easier to clean type of braces
- More comfort than traditional metal braces

Self-ligating braces are, on average, slightly more expensive than conventional metal braces. Also, because they don't use additional ligatures to fix the wires to each bracket, there are fewer risks of bacterial infections and the associated costs of treatment.

### [Invisalign](/treatments/invisalign/adult/)

Invisalign is an innovative transparent invisible alignment system. The major advantages of Invisalign are that it is barely perceptible, and you can remove your trays, allowing you to have better oral hygiene.

The significant improvements and design innovations make the costs of Invisalign to be higher than traditional stainless steel options and self-ligating braces.

Fortunately, as we'll see later, there are many resources at your disposal that keep upfront prices down and make the price differences almost negligible. In orthodontics, your options and preferences will usually align with your budget, letting you choose your desired alternative.
