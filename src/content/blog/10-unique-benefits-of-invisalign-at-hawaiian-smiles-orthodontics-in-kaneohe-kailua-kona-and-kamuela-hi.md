Maintaining oral hygiene during orthodontic treatment can be quite challenging, especially with traditional braces. The removable aspect of Invisalign becomes the star of the show again, allowing patients a smooth journey to keep their teeth and gums healthy. You can brush and floss as usual without any obstructions, reducing the risk of plaque buildup, tooth decay, and gum disease.
