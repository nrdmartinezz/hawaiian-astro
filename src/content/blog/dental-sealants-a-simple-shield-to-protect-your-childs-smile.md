At **Hawaiian Smiles Orthodontics** in beautiful Kaneohe, Hawaii, we believe prevention is the cornerstone of lifelong oral health. One of the most effective yet straightforward ways to protect your child’s teeth from decay is through dental sealants—an easy, painless treatment that acts as a protective barrier on the back teeth where cavities are most common.

### What Exactly Are Dental Sealants?

Dental sealants are thin coatings made from a safe, tooth-colored resin that are painted onto the chewing surfaces of molars and premolars. These teeth have natural grooves and pits that tend to trap food and bacteria, making them hard to clean thoroughly with brushing alone. Sealants fill in these crevices, creating a smooth surface that blocks out cavity-causing germs.

The process is quick and comfortable. Dr. Satya Nayak or Dr. Yim will clean and dry your child’s tooth, apply a gentle etching solution to help the sealant adhere, then carefully brush the liquid resin onto the tooth’s surface. A special curing light hardens the sealant in seconds. This protective shield can last for years and is checked at every visit to ensure it’s still intact.

### Why Sealants Matter: The Facts You Should Know

Research and public health organizations, including the **Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)**, confirm the powerful benefits of dental sealants. They can reduce the risk of cavities by up to **80%** on the back teeth, which is where most childhood cavities occur. In fact, children without sealants are nearly **three times more likely to develop cavities** than those with sealants in place.

Unfortunately, many families don’t realize how valuable sealants are or mistakenly think brushing alone is enough. At Hawaiian Smiles Orthodontics, we make sure every parent is informed about this simple preventative measure that can save your child from painful and costly dental work.

### When Should Sealants Be Applied?

Timing is key to maximize protection. Sealants work best when applied shortly after the permanent molars and premolars come in. Here’s a general guideline for when to consider sealants:

| Tooth Type              | Typical Age of Eruption | Ideal Time for Sealants     |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------- | --------------------------- |
| First Permanent Molars  | 5 to 7 years            | As soon as they fully erupt |
| Premolars               | 9 to 11 years           | Right after eruption        |
| Second Permanent Molars | 11 to 13 years          | Immediately after eruption  |

For children at higher risk of cavities, even baby molars can benefit from sealants to provide extra defense during early childhood.

Sealants are sturdy and long-lasting but are always monitored during regular checkups and can be reapplied if needed. Compared to the expense and discomfort of fillings or crowns, sealants offer a cost-effective and painless way to keep your child’s smile healthy.
