Both Invisalign and braces move teeth with planned, gentle force. The better choice depends on your bite, how you like to eat and brush, and how much you want the appliance to show.

## How they differ day to day

**Invisalign** uses a series of clear, removable trays. You take them out to eat and brush, then wear them 20–22 hours a day. Trays are smooth, so they are easy on cheeks and lips. We are an Invisalign Diamond Plus provider, which means a high volume of cases and access to the newest aligner features.

**Braces** stay on until the job is done. That control is useful for complex rotations, large gaps, or bite work that needs precise three-dimensional movement. Self-ligating brackets skip elastic ties, which means less plaque trap and often lighter forces. Kids who want personality can pick colorful bracket styles on our [braces](/treatments/braces/) page.

## Which one is “faster”?

Time depends more on the case than the appliance. Mild crowding can finish in about a year with either. A full bite correction may take 18–24 months. We will give you a range at the consult, not a sales pitch.

## Comfort, food, and emergencies

Aligners come out for meals, so there is no banned-food list — only the discipline to put them back in. Braces ask you to skip sticky and very hard foods, and a popped bracket can mean an extra visit. Our offices handle both kinds of urgent visits.
