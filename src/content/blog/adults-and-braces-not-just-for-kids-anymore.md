The word “braces” still sends a lot of adults straight back to middle school. Crooked teeth, a bite that never got finished, or a retainer that lived in a drawer for ten years are much more common reasons to start now. At [Hawaiian Smiles Orthodontics](/treatments/adult-treatment/), [Dr. Satya Nayak, Dr. Alyssa Shaikh, and Dr. Nicole Fernandez](/about/doctors/) treat adults in Kaneohe, Kailua-Kona, Kamuela, and Hilo with the same appliances we use for teens.

## Why adults start

Some people never had treatment as kids and are tired of hiding a smile. Others finished braces years ago, stopped wearing a retainer, and watched the teeth drift. Age is not the limit. Healthy teeth and gums are. A consult tells us whether anything needs attention from your dentist first, and which appliance fits the bite and your week.

## What we can use

**Self-ligating braces** are the workhorse for crowding, rotations, and bite changes that need precise control. We use [Pitts21 brackets](/treatments/braces/), which hold the wire with a small door instead of elastic ties. Visits are shorter, the brackets are easier to keep clean, and many adults finish sooner than they would have with older brace systems.

**Ceramic braces** are the choice when you want that same control with brackets that blend into the teeth. They handle crooked teeth and bite problems, and they cost more than metal. Dark drinks, red wine, coffee, and smoking can stain the adhesive around the brackets, so we talk through that before you choose them.

**Invisalign** is a series of clear, custom trays rather than brackets. You take them out to eat and brush, and most people around you will not notice them. As an [Invisalign Diamond Plus provider](/treatments/invisalign/adult/), we use aligners for a wide range of adult cases, including bites that used to mean braces only. Some movements still do better in brackets. We will say so if that is your case, instead of forcing one appliance.
